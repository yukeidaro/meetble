# Meetble: one-line pitch

**Other AI agents tell you who someone is; Meetble tells you how to actually reach them by your deadline, ranks every route by its odds, and does it for you.**

Alternates:
- "Give it a name and a deadline. It finds the door that's open, tells you the odds, and walks you through it."
- "We pointed it at tonight's judge. Here's what it found."
